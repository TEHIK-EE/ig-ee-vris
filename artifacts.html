Observation profiles

Observation: EE VRIS Attempting to Concieve

Observation profile for how long is woman attempting to concieve. (ee Rasestuda üritamine.)
Observation: EE VRIS Biochemical Pregnancy

Biochemical pregnancy detected by blood test. (ee Biokeemiline rasedus, tuvastatud vereanalüüsiga.)
Observation: EE VRIS Birth Weight

Birth weight of newborn child. (ee Lapse sünnikaal grammides.)
Observation: EE VRIS Blood Group

Observation profile for recording the ABO and Rh(D) blood group in the Estonian VRIS fertility system. (ee Veregrupp.)
Observation: EE VRIS Clinical Pregnancy

Clinical pregnancy, confirmed by ultrasound AND number of fetus. (ee Kliiniline rasedus, kinnitatud ultraheliuuringuga. LOOTEMUNADE arv ka! NB! Biokeemilise raseduse profiil on eraldi.)
Observation: EE VRIS Education Level

Education level of a donor or recipient in the Estonian VRIS fertility system. (ee Haridustase. NB! Pärimisel kasutada MPI profiili, sisestamisel või kui patsienti pole rahvastikuregistris, kasuta VRIS profiili.)
Observation: EE VRIS Embryo Lifecycle Counts

Summary counts of embryo lifecycle events in a fertility treatment cycle (frozen, transferred, destroyed). (ee Embrüote elutsükli kokkuvõtted: külmutatud, siirdatud, hävitatud arv tsüklis.)
Observation: EE VRIS Fertilization Counts

Counts of oocytes used and fertilized during fertilization procedure. (ee Viljastamise protseduuri munarakkude arvud erinevates staadiumites.)
Observation: EE VRIS Fetus Reduction

Observation profile for recording whether fetus reduction procedure was performed in multifetal pregnancy. (ee Loote/loodete reduktsiooni läbiviimine mitmikrasedusel.)
Observation: EE VRIS Height

Observation profile for recording height of a donor in the Estonian VRIS fertility system. (ee Pikkus.)
Observation: EE VRIS Marital Status

Marital status of a donor or recipient in the Estonian VRIS fertility system. (ee Perekonnaseis. NB! Pärimisel kasutada MPI profiili, sisestamisel või kui patsienti pole rahvastikuregistris, kasuta VRIS profiili.)
Observation: EE VRIS Neonatal Intensive Care

Whether the child received neonatal intensive care during first 7 days of life. (ee Lapse intensiivravi esimese 7 elupäeva jooksul.)
Observation: EE VRIS Obstetric History

Past obstetric history at the START of fertility treatment cycle. Records cumulative count of all prior pregnancies, deliveries, live births and stillbirths BEFORE current treatment. (ee Sünnitusanamnees ENNE käesolevat viljatusravi tsüklit. Varasemate raseduste, sünnituste ja sündide kumulatiivne arv.)
Observation: EE VRIS Ovarian Stimulation Protocol

Captures ovarian stimulation protocol details for fertility treatment cycle. (ee Munasarjade stimulatsiooni protokoll sisaldab meetodi, supressiooni, gonadotropiini ja eelneva ravi info.)
Observation: EE VRIS Physical Appearance

Observation profile for recording characteristics of physical appearance of donor. (ee Profiil on mõeldud välimust kirjeldavate karakteristikute jaoks, nt silmade värd, juuste värv, naha värv, kehaehitus.)
Observation: EE VRIS Pregnancy Outcome

Pregnancy outcome including delivery, miscarriage, abortion details. (ee Raseduse lõpe sünnitus, katkemine või katkestamine koos detailidega.)
Observation: EE VRIS Prenatal Care Discontinued

Indicator whether prenatal care was discontinued by patient or for unknown reasons. (ee Rasedusaegne tervisekontroll katkestatud patsiendi poolt või teadmata põhjusel.)
Observation: EE VRIS Prior Fertility Treatment

Prior fertility treatment history. Records whether IVF and/or IUI has been performed and the number of cycles. (ee Varasema viljatusravi anamnees)
Observation: EE VRIS Stimulation Protocol for Embryo Transfer

Captures stimulation protocol details for thawed embryo. (ee Sulatatud embrüo siirdamise protokoll jm.)
Observation: EE VRIS Tobacco Use

Observation profile for tobacco use status in the Estonian VRIS fertility system. (ee Tubakatoodete kasutamine.)
Observation: EE VRIS Waist

Observation profile for recording waist circumference of a donor in the Estonian VRIS fertility system. (ee Vööümbermõõt.)
Observation: EE VRIS Weight

Observation profile for recording weight of a donor in the Estonian VRIS fertility system. (ee Kaal.)

Behavior: Operation Definitions

These are custom operations that can be supported by and/or invoked by systems conforming to this implementation guide.

$patient-observations operation

TEST Custom operation to retrieve all Observation resources for a specific patient in Estonian VRIS. Supports optional filtering by profile, category, code, and date range. (ee TEST Operatioon kõikide Observation ressursside pärimiseks VRIS-ist. Tagastab Bundle'i kõikide Observationitega, kus subject viitab antud patsiendile.)
